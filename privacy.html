Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Video Privacy (Zero Cloud Uploads)

AI Gaming Shorts Studio is a local desktop application. Your video files, audio tracks, and rendered exports are processed entirely on your local machine using your own CPU/GPU. We never upload, stream, view, or store your gameplay videos on our servers.

2. Information We Collect

To manage authentication and quotas, our cloud server stores only: (a) your registered email address, (b) an anonymized hardware Machine GUID to prevent trial abuse, and (c) subscription status and daily usage counts.

3. Payment Data

We do not collect or store your payment card numbers or banking credentials. All payment processing is managed by our Merchant of Record in compliance with PCI-DSS standards.

4. Contact

Questions regarding privacy can be directed to support@aigamingshorts.com.
